## Research question and method

The rapid review asks how generative AI is integrated into mental-health apps for people aged 11 to 24 and which benefits and disadvantages are reported. Four databases were searched for studies published from 2022 to 2025, yielding five included studies.

## Main findings

Chatbots and conversational interfaces were the main integration form. Included studies indicate usability and engagement and limited possible benefits for anxiety and depression, especially among participants with elevated symptoms, but preventive and health-promoting evidence remains weak.

## Assessment relevance

`Generative_KI` is central through generative conversational apps. `KI_Sonstige` and `Diversitaet` are relevant to digital mental-health access and youth populations. The review does not directly study social work, prompting or demographic fairness.

## Limitations

Only five heterogeneous studies were included, usually with small samples and short follow-up. AI architectures were inconsistently reported, screening was only partly duplicated, and long-term benefits and harms remain unclear.
