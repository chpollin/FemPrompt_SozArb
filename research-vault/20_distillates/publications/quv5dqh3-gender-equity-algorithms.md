## Core statements

- It connects these mechanisms to unequal service quality, resource allocation, stereotypes, erasure and physical or health harms. ^s2

## Method and scope

The publication is an expert synthesis and action framework rather than a new controlled study. It draws on documented cases and on the authors' tracking of around 133 publicly reported biased machine-learning systems across industries. The article reports that 59 of those systems demonstrated gender bias and organizes observed impacts into six types. It then gives three actions for social-change leaders and four for machine-learning developers. These include filling gender data gaps, building AI literacy among gender experts, examining team and data composition, documenting datasets and models, conducting gender-lens audits, participatory design and governance with gender diversity.
