# Distillate: Algorithmic management in a work context

The conceptual article treats algorithmic management as a sociotechnical arrangement shaped by technical infrastructure and organisational choice. It analyses power, competencies, resistance and opacity across standard and platform work.

## Research question and method

The article asks how algorithmic management enters ordinary as well as platform work and reshapes organisational power, roles, competencies and information exchange. It is a conceptual sociotechnical analysis of existing research rather than a new worker survey.

## Main findings

The analysis argues that algorithms can expand managerial surveillance and control while also redistributing managerial authority. Workers need algorithmic competencies and may respond with aversion or complacency. Technical and organisational opacity jointly constrain knowledge and accountability.

## Limitations
