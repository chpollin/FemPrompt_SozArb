# Distillate: Ethics & AI: A systematic review on ethical concerns and related strategies for designing with AI in healthcare

The systematic review maps ethical concerns and design strategies for artificial intelligence in healthcare. It identifies twelve main issues and nineteen sub-issues across the included literature.

## Core statements

- The review organises healthcare AI ethics into a broad issue taxonomy that includes justice and fairness, autonomy, privacy, transparency, safety, trust, responsibility and dignity. ^s1
  > "The review, which followed the PRISMA guidelines, revealed 12 main ethical  issues" (10.3390/ai4010003)

- The synthesis rests on a defined search and full-text selection process. ^s2
  > "This eventually resulted in 45 documents which were subjected a thorough full-text analysis" (10.3390/ai4010003)

## Research question and method

The review asks which ethical issues and related strategies are identified for AI in healthcare. It searches academic databases and a guideline inventory, then uses thematic code mapping on the included documents.

## Main arguments

Ethical design cannot be reduced to model accuracy. Healthcare AI also implicates autonomy, consent, privacy, explainability, security, responsibility, solidarity and conflicts among stakeholders. The paper links each issue to strategies reported in the reviewed literature.

## Assessment relevance

## Limitations

The search covers literature available through September 2020 and includes heterogeneous academic and grey-literature guidance. The taxonomy describes reported concerns and strategies rather than testing their effectiveness.
