# Distillate: Artificial intelligence in social work: An EPIC model for practice

The literature-based article proposes EPIC for ethically integrating AI into social work.

## Core statements

- EPIC combines ethics and justice, policy and advocacy, intersectoral collaboration, and community engagement and empowerment. ^s1
  > "The model consists of four components: (E) ethics and justice; (P) policy development and  advocacy;  (I)  intersectoral  collaboration;  and  (C)  community  engagement  and empowerment" (10.1080/0312407x.2025.2488345)

- The framework advocates proactive ethical governance. ^s2
  > "proactive and ethical approach towards a more secure, safe, transparent, and socially just future." (10.1080/0312407x.2025.2488345)

## Research question and method

The article reviews literature on AI and social work and derives a practice framework. It does not evaluate EPIC in practice.

## Assessment relevance

Social work, AI, bias and fairness are central through its profession-specific governance model.

## Limitations

The conceptual model requires empirical implementation research with practitioners and service users.
