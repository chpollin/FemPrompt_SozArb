## Research question and method

The randomized controlled study asks whether Emohaa reduces mental distress and whether adding its generative emotional-support bot complements structured cognitive behavioural exercises. Participants were assigned to CBT-Bot, the combined system or control, with intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses.

## Main findings

Compared with control, both intervention groups improved on depression, negative affect and insomnia. Anxiety was not significant under the stricter intention-to-treat analysis, and positive affect did not show an intervention effect.

## Limitations

The intervention was short, assessments were limited, and control-group follow-up was absent. The design does not isolate ES-Bot efficacy from the CBT component or establish long-term clinical effectiveness.
