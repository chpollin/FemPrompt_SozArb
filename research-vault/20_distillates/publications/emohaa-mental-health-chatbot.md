# Distillate: A chatbot for mental health support: Exploring the impact of Emohaa on reducing mental distress in China

The randomized controlled study evaluates a Chinese conversational agent that combines structured cognitive behavioural exercises with generative emotional support. Reported improvements concern depression, negative affect and insomnia, while the short study and limited follow-up constrain inference.

## Research question and method

The randomized controlled study asks whether Emohaa reduces mental distress and whether adding its generative emotional-support bot complements structured cognitive behavioural exercises. Participants were assigned to CBT-Bot, the combined system or control, with intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses.

## Main findings

Compared with control, both intervention groups improved on depression, negative affect and insomnia. Anxiety was not significant under the stricter intention-to-treat analysis, and positive affect did not show an intervention effect.

## Assessment relevance

`Generative_KI` is relevant because ES-Bot generates emotional-support dialogue. `KI_Sonstige` is relevant because the combined intervention includes structured cognitive behavioural exercises delivered by CBT-Bot. The study does not examine prompt engineering or input design. It concerns mental-health support, but it does not study social-work practice or fairness.

## Limitations

The intervention was short, assessments were limited, and control-group follow-up was absent. The design does not isolate ES-Bot efficacy from the CBT component or establish long-term clinical effectiveness.
