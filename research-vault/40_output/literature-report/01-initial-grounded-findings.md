## Prompting and bias mitigation

Kaneko et al. report lower measured bias scores for zero-shot and few-shot Chain-of-Thought prompting in their evaluation.[^2] The cited evaluations report that the effects of prompting-based bias mitigation vary across model conditions and bias categories.[^3]
