# Guidance for social-work research proposes disclosing the prompts and the language model used

## Statement

Two conceptual papers on generative AI in social-work research propose that researchers disclose the prompts they used together with the identity of the language model, and one of them also requires its version.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s3]] supports reporting of tasks, prompts, tool names and version numbers for AI-assisted analysis.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s3]] supports disclosure of the system, the prompts, the location of use, conflicts of interest and limitations.

## Conditions and limits

Both papers are conceptual and normative contributions from 2023 addressed to social-work science, according to the method and limitations sections of their distillates. Neither evaluates whether disclosure improves reproducibility or research quality. The recommendations concern research use, not practice with service users.
