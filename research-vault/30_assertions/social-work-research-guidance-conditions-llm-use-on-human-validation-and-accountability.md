# Social-work research guidance conditions LLM use on human validation and researcher accountability

## Statement

Patton et al. (2023) and Victor et al. (2023) recommend that material produced or edited with LLMs in social-work research be validated by humans before dissemination and that researchers examine, verify and take accountability for the text and analyses these tools produce.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s2]] supports the recommendation of human validation before AI-edited qualitative material enters professional or scientific dissemination.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s1]] supports the recommendation that researchers examine, verify and take accountability for text and analyses produced with generative AI.

## Scope boundary

Both statements are recommendations of their sources, formulated in 2023 for social-work research and not for practice with service users. They are not recommendations of this review. Neither source tests whether human validation detects or prevents the errors it targets. For SQ1 the Assertion records the condition under which these sources consider the uses in [[30_assertions/patton-and-victor-describe-prospective-llm-uses-in-social-work-research]] acceptable. Contradicting sources were not systematically searched, so `contested-with: []` does not assert consensus.
