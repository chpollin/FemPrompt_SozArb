# Prompt language shifted rather than removed measured cultural bias

## Statement

A study of multi-agent cultural debate reports that prompting in Chinese shifted measured cultural bias toward East Asian perspectives rather than eliminating it.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/multi-agent-cultural-debate#^s1]]. The experiment separates prompt-language effects from culturally explicit multi-agent deliberation.

## Scope boundary

The benchmark covers five cultural domains with discrete labels, and the study did not repeat multi-run evaluations. The Assertion concerns the prompt-language condition only and makes no claim about the effect of the multi-agent method.
