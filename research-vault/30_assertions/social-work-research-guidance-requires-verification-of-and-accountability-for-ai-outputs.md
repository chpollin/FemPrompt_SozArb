# Social-work research guidance requires verification of and accountability for AI outputs

## Statement

Two guidance articles for social-work research require that users verify generated text and analyses and remain accountable for them before professional or scientific use.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s1]]. Victor et al. (2023) state that researchers must examine, verify and take accountability for text and analyses produced with generative AI.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s2]]. Patton et al. (2023) require human validation before AI-edited qualitative material enters professional or scientific dissemination.

## Scope boundary

Both sources address social-work research and publishing, not direct practice with service users. They are normative recommendations from 2023 and report no evaluation of how verification is carried out or how well it detects errors or bias. Transfer to practice settings is an open question for the synthesis.
