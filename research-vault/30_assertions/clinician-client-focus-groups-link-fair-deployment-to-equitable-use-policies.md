# Focus groups of clinicians who are also therapy clients link fair deployment of an AI tool to equitable use policies

## Statement

In focus groups with people who were both mental-health clinicians and current therapy clients, fair deployment of a hypothetical AI deception-detection tool was tied to equitable use policies that address who is subjected to the tool, which authority selects them and whether discretionary use creates further stigma or power imbalance.
