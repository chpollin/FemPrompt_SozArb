# Focus groups anticipate misreading of underrepresented clients by AI deception detection

## Statement

Participants in focus groups on a hypothetical AI deception-detection tool for mental-health care warned that culturally specific, neurodivergent or emotionally regulated behaviour could be misread as deception and that demographically skewed training data could produce higher false-positive rates for clients from underrepresented backgrounds.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/9ep4ic4t-deception-detection-mental-health#^s2]] supports both warnings, quoting that such systems may struggle to interpret nonverbal and paralinguistic cues of clients from underrepresented backgrounds.

## Scope boundary

Affected groups: therapy clients from underrepresented cultural backgrounds, neurodivergent clients and clients with emotionally regulated behaviour. System: a hypothetical multimodal deception-detection system, not a generative LLM. Study type: qualitative focus-group study, whose participants were clinicians who were also current therapy clients according to the distillate's method section. The harms are anticipated by participants and are not measured.
