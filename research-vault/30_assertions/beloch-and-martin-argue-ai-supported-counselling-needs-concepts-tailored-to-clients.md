# Beloch and Martin argue that AI-supported counselling needs concepts tailored to clients

## Statement

From findings on digital social and debt counselling, Beloch and Martin (2026) argue that AI-supported counselling would need counselling concepts geared to the resources and needs of clients.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/generative-ai-social-debt-counselling#^s1]] supports the transfer argument and its orientation to client resources and needs.
- [[20_distillates/publications/generative-ai-social-debt-counselling#^s3]] limits the argument, because the study collected no chatbot-specific data.

## Scope boundary

The empirical basis concerns digital and hybrid counselling, not chatbot use. The Assertion records a prospective transfer argument and makes no claim about chatbot uptake, effectiveness or safety.
