# Social-work research guidance requires disclosure of prompts and models

## Statement

Two guidance articles for social-work research treat prompts as part of the research record and require disclosure of the model used and its prompts, and one adds tool versions for reproducible reporting.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s3]]. Victor et al. (2023) require reporting of tasks, prompts, tool names and version numbers for LLM-assisted analysis.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s3]]. Patton et al. (2023) require disclosure of the system, prompts, location of use, conflicts and limitations.

## Scope boundary

The requirement concerns research transparency. In these sources prompting competence appears as a documentation duty, not as a technique for improving outputs. The sources do not test whether disclosure is sufficient for reproducibility.
