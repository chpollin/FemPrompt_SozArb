# A counterfactual gender audit is demonstrated on language-model summaries of adult social-care records

## Statement

A study of language-model summaries of long-term-care case notes demonstrates a counterfactual audit that varies the represented gender in otherwise constant care records and is designed to show both whether and in which form generated summaries differ.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-j4k3xa52-gender-bias-long-term-care-llms#^s1]] supports the counterfactual design that holds the care record constant. This statement carries no quotation in the distillate.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-j4k3xa52-gender-bias-long-term-care-llms#^s4]] supports the aim of showing presence and form of bias and the authors' claim of a reproducible framework.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-j4k3xa52-gender-bias-long-term-care-llms#^s2]] supports the application result that models differed, with one model showing no detected gender differences and another the clearest differences.

## Conditions and limits

The distillate's method section names pseudonymised adult social-care records from one English local authority and summaries generated by Llama 3, Gemma, T5 and BART at several output lengths. Its limitations section records one service domain, inputs shorter than full case histories, removal of cases with gender-specific circumstances, many word-level tests and no observation of downstream allocation decisions. The audit detects differences and does not itself mitigate them.
