# Rickman proposes a counterfactual evaluation of gender bias in generated care summaries

## Statement

Rickman (2025) proposes a counterfactual evaluation that holds care records constant while varying the represented gender, designed to expose both the presence and the form of gender bias in LLM-generated care summaries.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-j4k3xa52-gender-bias-long-term-care-llms#^s1]] supports the counterfactual pair design.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-j4k3xa52-gender-bias-long-term-care-llms#^s4]] supports the aim of exposing presence and form of bias rather than a single score.

## Scope boundary

The records come from one local authority and one adult social-care domain, and the study compares men and women only. The source presents an evaluation method. It does not frame the method as an organisational procedure for services, and that use would be an inference of the synthesis.
