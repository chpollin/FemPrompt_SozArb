# Gender-equity and feminist contributions reject algorithmic neutrality

## Statement

A practice-oriented gender-equity article and a feminist conference chapter locate bias in socially constructed decisions about development teams, data, labels, variables and proxies, and reject the premise that algorithmic systems are neutral.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/quv5dqh3-gender-equity-algorithms#^s1]] supports the location of gender bias in decisions about development teams, data collection and labelling, training sets, variables and proxies.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-v8sbeh4e-feminist-ai-neutrality#^s1]] supports the framing of algorithmic neutrality as an illusion because data and design are socially constructed.

## Scope boundary

Affected groups: women, with marginalised groups named in the feminist chapter. Systems: machine-learning and AI systems in general, not LLMs specifically. Study types: an expert synthesis and action framework without a reproducible case-selection protocol, and a conceptual chapter with a small local attitude survey. The Assertion states where the sources locate the origin of bias. It makes no empirical claim about the size of gender bias in any system. `contested-with: []` records that no conflicting statement was found among the active distillates and does not assert consensus.
