# Guidance for social-work research requires human verification of generated text and analyses

## Statement

Two conceptual papers on generative AI in social-work research propose human verification of generated text and analyses, one as part of the researchers' accountability for the results and the other as a condition before AI-edited qualitative material enters professional or scientific dissemination.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s1]] supports the duty to inspect and verify outputs and the researchers' accountability.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s2]] supports human validation of AI-edited qualitative material before dissemination.
