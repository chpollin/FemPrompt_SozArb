# Statistical fairness criteria leave the rights questions of child-protection decisions open

## Statement

A critical analysis of predictive algorithms in child protection argues that statistical fairness criteria do not settle the rights questions of due process, participation, consent and the right to challenge an inference, which remain relevant because interventions concern identifiable families.
