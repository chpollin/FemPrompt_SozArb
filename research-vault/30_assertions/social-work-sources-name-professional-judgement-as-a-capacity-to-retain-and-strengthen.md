# Social-work sources name professional judgement as a capacity to retain and strengthen

## Statement

Social-work sources name professional judgement as a capacity that education should strengthen and that AI-supported work should remain subordinate to.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/vszm7ct6-ai-social-work-education#^s3]]. Hodgson et al. (2022) identify critical thinking, judgement, appraisal and creativity as educational emphases where automated systems may take over routine functions.
- [[20_distillates/publications/social-work-students-ai-readiness#^s2]]. The source calls for curricula that join technical understanding with professional judgement.
- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-zitlbm8a-chatgpt-social-work-science#^s1]]. Patton et al. (2023) state that ChatGPT should remain subordinate to contextual human judgement in social-work research.

## Scope boundary

The sources are a prospective conceptual discussion, a student attitude survey with curricular conclusions and an invited commentary on social-work research. They name judgement as a requirement. They do not define or measure it, and none tests whether judgement corrects errors or biases in generated output. Contradicting sources were not systematically searched.
