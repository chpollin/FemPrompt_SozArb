# Victor et al. require knowledge of tool construction, limits and biases

## Statement

Victor et al. (2023) state that responsible use of generative AI in social-work research requires knowledge of a tool's construction, training data, limitations and potential biases.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/completion-amyzfaph-generative-ai-social-work-research#^s2]]. The source names this knowledge as the basis for recognising limitations and biases and for choosing a suitable tool.

## Scope boundary

The statement is a normative requirement for researchers. The source does not specify the depth of technical knowledge required and does not evaluate whether such knowledge changes research quality. Contradicting sources were not systematically searched.
