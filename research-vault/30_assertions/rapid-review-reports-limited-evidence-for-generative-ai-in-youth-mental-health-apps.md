# A rapid review reports limited evidence for generative AI in youth mental-health apps

## Statement

The 2026 rapid review "Generative AI in Youth Mental Health Apps" finds a small, heterogeneous evidence base dominated by chatbots and concludes that evidence for preventive or health-promoting effects of generative AI in mental-health apps for young people remains limited.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/generative-ai-youth-mental-health-apps#^s1]] supports the small number of included studies and the dominance of chatbots.
- [[20_distillates/publications/generative-ai-youth-mental-health-apps#^s2]] supports the conclusion that evidence for preventive or health-promoting effects remains limited.
