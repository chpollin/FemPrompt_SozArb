# Kabra et al. transfer reasoning traces through fine-tuning

## Statement

Kabra, Jha and Reddy define ReGiFT as a fine-tuning procedure that transfers reasoning traces generated by a reasoning model to base models as training supervision.

## Support

- [[20_distillates/publications/kabra-2025-reasoning-guided-fine-tuning#^s1]] supports the generation and use of traces for training.
- [[20_distillates/publications/kabra-2025-reasoning-guided-fine-tuning#^s2]] supports the distinction from inference-time Chain-of-Thought prompting alone.

## Scope boundary

This Assertion describes the procedure proposed in the paper. It claims neither that every reasoning trace is correct or faithful to the model's actual process nor that this training generally reduces bias.
