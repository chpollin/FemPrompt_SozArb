# Pilot paper A: generative models in social work education

## Introduction

This synthetic full text exists only for the PRISM browser pilot. It is not a real publication and carries no copyrighted content.

Generative language models are entering social work education through writing support, case simulation and feedback tools. Educators report that the generated case vignettes tend to reproduce gendered scripts of care, assigning emotional labour to women and decision authority to men.

## Method

Twelve simulated classroom sessions were coded for the distribution of roles in generated vignettes. The coding followed a closed vocabulary.

## Findings

Across the sessions the models reproduced gendered scripts in the majority of vignettes. Prompts that named the bias explicitly reduced the effect but did not remove it.

## Discussion

The finding motivates evidence-grounded screening: a reviewer must be able to point at the sentence that justifies a category, which is what this pilot exercises.
