## Abstract

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a crucial force that is shaping and influencing our society in modern world. The algorithm that drives these systems are not neutral with biases data that are been increasingly questioned. The feminists critique the algorithms of AI and expose the myth of algorithm to be neutral, instead it reflects the biases, power dynamics and social disparities inherent in their design. Through a feminists lens, this paper examines the inequalities perpetuate be AI technologies and how feminists methodologies and theories such as cyberfeminism becomes a vision and also a tool to reimagine and refine AI development and hoe it can transform and help to eliminate these biases, the research advocates for more inclusive, transparent and ethically responsible AI systems that challenges traditional power, exclusionary structure and calls for a redefinition of AI that serves inclusivity without reinforcing existing prejudice. ## 3.1 Gender bias in AI system

Toupin [2023], it shows how in sectors like health care and recruitment algorithms often perpetuates systemic inequalities leading disadvantages for women and how feminist approaches in AI not only talks about fixing the problems of biasness but also about thinking how AI is used. Similarly, Lee &amp; Kim [2021] found that recruitment algorithms favour male candidates more which shows and reinforces inequalities on the bases of historical data and highlights the importance of feminist critique in AI system.

## 3.2 Questioning the Fantasy of Neutrality in Algorithm

Rahmatullah &amp; Gupta [2023] has shown AI gendered algorithms are deeply ingrained in with far -reaching societal impacts, requiring for a robust interdisciplinary response deep in cybernetic feminism as both a methodological tool for analysis and an honest structure for AI development and achieve a truly equitable digital outlook. Similarly, D'lgnazio &amp; Klein [2020] introduced Data feminism which

## 3.3 Intersectionality in AI development

Intersectionality reveals how covering on identities such a gender, race, class effect on how individuals experience AI. For example, O' Connor &amp; Liu [2023] shows how AI can increase existing gender biases but it also offers opportunities and for reducing these issues requires a broad approach to intersectional consideration in AI development.

## 3.4 Inclusivity in AI development

Scrutinize the dataset and algorithm to reduce biases and improve equality in AI work. Oxford university press [2023] talks about a critical interdisciplinary feminist lens for understanding and shaping the development of AI. It emphasizes on the need for AI to be more inclusive and accountable and to actively contribute in social justice while providing a collective or inclusive environment for diverse perspectives on these technologies.

## 4.9 Discussion

The findings shows that AI is not neutral in fact they often perpetuate social biases and inequalities as they have been created by data controlled by human and historical biases. Feminist approaches help us to fix problems by focusing on different people experiences. One of the findings is multiple overlapping of identities which is often ignored in AI researches.

Feminist perspectives in this becomes a powerful tool to address these issues. It emphasizes on ethics and understanding how AI has an impact on different  people.  Another  is  lack  of  diversity  in  the  development  of  AI teams. When most of the people who build AI are from the similar groups, they tend to overlook certain biases whose outcome is unfairness in the data. The lack of diversity sets limitation in the perspective and it calls for creation of inclusive AI system inclusion of more voices of marginalized groups  and  women  would  help  in  inclusivity  in  the  system,  these  approaches not only critiques but becomes a path way by including co- design it helps in inclusivity and framework of accountability that go beyond transparency. Overall, it challenges the existing structure and suggest to build for a fair and ethical framework and ensure through feminist perspective that AI system promotes equality rather than reinforcing historical biases.
